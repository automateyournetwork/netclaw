# NetClaw 1.19.1

Spec: [180-hub-credential-renewal](../../specs/180-hub-credential-renewal/spec.md)

## Changes

- **The Border renews its hub credential.** The hub certificate the Border presents to
  its members is issued for 90 days, but NetClaw never reissued it. After 90 days
  members rejected the hub's attestation and stopped re-attaching on reconnect, and
  the rotation schedule's renewal of it always failed with "operator renewal is
  required".

  With the certificate lifecycle on (`N2N_CERT_MODE`), the Border now puts the hub
  credential on the rotation schedule, which renews it at 2/3 of its life. In every
  mode, a certificate within 7 days of expiry is renewed the next time a member asks
  for attestation. Renewal
  keeps the risk CA and the hub key, so members' pinned anchor and the hub key id
  (used by protected operations) do not change.

## Upgrade and compatibility

No action needed. A Border whose hub certificate has already expired renews it on
the next member reconnect after upgrading and restarting the mesh daemon. Members
need no change.

## Validation and known limitations

See [verification](../../specs/180-hub-credential-renewal/verification.md).

- Tests cover the expired-hub regression, key-preserving renewal, the scheduled path,
  and concurrent renewals; the full federation suite shows no new failures.
- A member channel that stays open across the old certificate's expiry closes then and
  the member reconnects with the renewed certificate.
- The on-demand renewal has run on an operator Border since 2026-10-23. A live
  renewal with protected operations enrolled has not been tested.
