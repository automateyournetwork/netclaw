Every cell names qualification scope and ingress cloud/region/AZ/node, receiving group, member/profile, expected tool, operator principal, original operation and result/ACK digests. A different remote group cannot satisfy the cell. At least one representative member belongs to each receiving group; Azure/GCP may use fresh local targets. No physical Azure/GCP placement claim follows. Matrix27 does not claim every member permutation; AWS9pair coverage is separately required at stage4.

Recovery is read-only: original operation/result retained and before/after approval/inference/effect counters equal. Device-key principals remain restricted; Mobile has no newly granted cross-cloud authority. Explicit manual member restart and artifact-reference limits remain unless improved and independently qualified. Guard/unknown-producing fault tests run after success cases, with no replacement operation used to erase an unknown.

The implemented offline verifier is `matrix.py`, exposed through qualification wrappers. It freezes signed group manifests against explicit anchor pins, exact member/profile/tool bindings,9or27cells and a finite campaign window. Each original assignment and HTTP capture has a separately retained digest. Verification checks original v2 approval/result/ACK signatures, receiving and accepting authority, durable member SQLite fields, native tool-evidence hash/counts, exact operation/digests and ingress attribution. Remote cells additionally verify the existing signed peer-client endorsement and receiving manifest. Local ingress attribution depends on the independently archived authenticated HTTP capture; result signatures alone do not prove the ingress path. No fixture result establishes live placement, quorum, successor completion, native recovery or overall acceptance.

Owner amendment: explicit `ohio-three-az` AWS group uses one observed region and three distinct observed zone IDs/instances. Azure/GCP still require three regions each. A successful matrix is not regional-outage or complete AZ-outage evidence. Original regional contracts remain available only in explicit/default `three-region` mode.
