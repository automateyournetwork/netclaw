## Objective and boundary

Prepare an evidence-led AWS extension of the qualified Border/member/HA/federation contracts. The next session must establish the actual account, region availability, existing inventory, billing and delegated budget before choosing infrastructure. This intake does not assume that the Azure or GCP driver can be copied without AWS-specific review.

The expected technical objective is protected AWS Border/member execution and three-region authority, then an explicitly scoped cross-cloud interoperability proof. Whether Azure/GCP are reactivated, which members are required, and the exact six-or-nine-ingress matrix remain consequential scope decisions. No previous cloud is retained or implicitly reactivated for this work.

- R199-06: Prove actual effects through every required ingress, including followers, physical cloud member placement, actual successor completion and native HUD/Code approval/recovery. Offline checks and catalogs alone cannot establish completion.

## Acceptance definition to finalize before activation

The reviewed matrix must identify cloud, region, ingress role, member placement, provider/tool profile, approval principal and expected result/ACK. It must include a new operation after an actual leader change, a stopped-guard fail-closed witness, native GET-only recovery with unchanged counters, final cost reconciliation and verified cleanup. Explicitly decide whether automatic member recovery and full old-leader artifact replication are required; Spec198 did not qualify those stronger behaviors.
